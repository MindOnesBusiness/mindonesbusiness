Why we are here

Big hopes. Then real life.

As a kid, you were told you would do big things. Then real life hit. Bills, stress, and people who did not get you. Now your mind races, but your days feel stuck. Many gifted adults feel this way. You are not alone.

Our name comes from America’s first coin, made in 1787. It said two things: Fugio, which means “time flies,” and Mind Your Business. Your time matters. So we skip the noise and focus on what you can do today.

We are a private club. Inside, you can learn about health, money, Bitcoin, contracts, setting up a business, and your feelings. You also meet people walking the same road.
