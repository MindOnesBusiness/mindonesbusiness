About the Guild

Inspired by America's first coin

In 1787, America made its very first coin. It had no king and no president on it. It had a sundial, a bright sun, and two messages. One was Fugio. It means “I fly,” as in time flies. The other was Mind Your Business.

MindOnesBusiness is built on that same idea. Your time is the one thing you can’t get back. We are a private membership association. That means we are a private club, not open to the public. We are for people who want to stop wasting their time on things that don’t help them, and use it to grow what they know, their health, their money, and their freedom.

Inside, you will learn about private contracts, trusts, keeping what you own safe, Bitcoin, health and detox, and shadow work (facing the feelings you hide). You will also meet people walking the same road.
