About the Guild

Inspired by America's first coin

In 1787, the first coin authorized by the United States carried no king, no president, and no slogan about obedience. It carried a sundial, a blazing sun, and two messages: Fugio, "I fly," meaning time flies, and Mind Your Business.

MindOnesBusiness is built on that same idea. Your time is the one asset you can't earn back. We are a private membership association for people who want to stop leaking it into systems that don't serve them, and start investing it in their own knowledge, health, wealth, and freedom.

Inside, you'll find education on private contracting, trusts and asset protection, financial sovereignty and Bitcoin, wellness and detox, and the inner work of shadow and emotional mastery. You'll also find people walking the same road.
